## What’s new and why it matters
I pulled two fresh Eurostat datasets for a short-video series and noticed they look suspiciously alike: AI in firms: share of enterprises with 10+ employees (excluding the financial sector) that use at least one AI technology, 2025 ( isoc_eb_ai ). Online shopping: share of people who bought something online in the last 12 months, 2025 ( isoc_ec_ib20 , indicator I_BLT12 ). Across the 27 EU countries, the two correlate with r = 0.70 (Spearman rank correlation also 0.70). Countries where almost everyone shops online tend to be the countries whose companies use AI.
