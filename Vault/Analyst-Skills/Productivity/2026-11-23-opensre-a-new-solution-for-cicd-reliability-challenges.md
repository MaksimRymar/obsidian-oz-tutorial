## What’s new and why it matters
The Specific Problem It Solves OpenSRE tackles the challenge of understanding CI failures, which often leave teams unsure of the cause and resolution. When a CI pipeline fails, it typically only indicates that something went wrong, without providing insights into why or how to fix it. OpenSRE is designed to investigate failures immediately, reading logs, testing hypotheses, and suggesting fixes for review, thus improving recovery time from red builds.
