## What’s new and why it matters
Ask three people in the same company "what were our sales last month?" and you may get three answers. Finance counts invoices issued. Sales counts orders confirmed. Operations counts what actually left the warehouse. Each number is right by its own rules. The trouble starts when two of them end up in the same meeting. Connect an AI assistant to those systems without agreed definitions, and it may use a different rule from the one in your finance report.
