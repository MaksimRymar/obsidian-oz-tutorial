## What’s new and why it matters
Most people who sell 3D prints guess the price. They add filament cost, double it, and hope. Then a failed print or a marketplace fee eats the profit. Here is a simple formula that covers the real costs. The formula cost = (filament + power + machine wear + labor) x (1 + failure allowance) price = cost x (1 + markup), then add the marketplace fee so it does not shrink your margin Each part Filament: grams used x price per gram.
