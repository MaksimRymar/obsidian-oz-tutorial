## What’s new and why it matters
The classic signal shows up: slow theta rhythms rise and alpha falls. Age and sex alone only reach about 59%, so the EEG is doing the work. But telling Alzheimer's from FTD is much harder: our best attempt (* a person-level model plus a second, eyes-open recording) reaches 63.3%, only a little above guessing. More people, not more features, is probably the fix. That's the open problem, and where I'd love help. Model selection uses nested cross-validation, so the numbers aren't tuned on test people. Honest caveats: a small single-site sample, and it's a research benchmark, not a diagnostic tool…
