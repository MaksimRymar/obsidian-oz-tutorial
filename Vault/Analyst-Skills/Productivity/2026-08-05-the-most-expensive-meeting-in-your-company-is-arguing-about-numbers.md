## What’s new and why it matters
The Most Expensive Meeting in Your Company Is the One Spent Arguing About the Numbers. Finance presents one set of KPIs. Operations presents another. Product has a different dashboard. Marketing reports different growth figures. Instead of making decisions, leadership spends the meeting debating whose numbers are “right.” This isn’t simply a reporting problem. It’s a TRUST problem. As organizations grow, each department gradually develops its own view of the business: • Marketing exports data into spreadsheets. • Finance builds reports around accounting systems. • Operations tracks performance…
