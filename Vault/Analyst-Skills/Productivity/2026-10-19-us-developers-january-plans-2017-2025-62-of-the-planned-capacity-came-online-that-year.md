## What’s new and why it matters
Every January, EIA's monthly generator inventory (EIA-860M) lists the generators their owners plan to bring online that year. I followed each January plan from 2017 to 2025 to what became of it, and every generator that came online back to where it stood in January. The checks were fixed before the run, and all of them passed. 62.3% came online in the planned year. Of 391.6 GW planned across the nine years, 243.8 GW came online in its year. 118.2 GW (30.2%) was still planned for a later month, and only 7.9 GW (2.0%) dropped out of the listings.
