## What’s new and why it matters
Hi! This post is to announce the birth & launch of AegisVPN! You see, I was fed up of having to pay for a VPN so I made my own! Introducing AegisVPN! A VPN that hops Tor servers all around the world to provide free and unlimited data, the server is hosted locally on your device for security and tunnel is encrypted by protocols like WireGaurd and OpenVPN and Stable DPI Obscufaction etc, along with offering basically every country in the world, along with a host of other features, it was currently tested on MacOS.
