## What’s new and why it matters
Most people picture robotics as a metal arm in a factory or a humanoid doing backflips in a viral video. The part nobody sees is the code. A robot, no matter how expensive its hardware is, is just a pile of motors and sensors until someone writes the instructions that tell it what to do, when to do it and what to do when something goes wrong. That is what robotics programming is. And it is a lot more approachable and a lot more useful for everyday business than most people think. So, what is robotics programming?
