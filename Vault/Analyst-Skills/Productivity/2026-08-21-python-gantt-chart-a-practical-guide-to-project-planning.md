## What’s new and why it matters

A plain task list rarely shows those relationships clearly. That confusion creates missed handoffs, overloaded team members, and meetings spent reconstructing what should happen next. Even a small project can become difficult to manage when timing remains hidden. A Python Gantt chart gives you a visual timeline for tasks, durations, dependencies, milestones, and progress.
