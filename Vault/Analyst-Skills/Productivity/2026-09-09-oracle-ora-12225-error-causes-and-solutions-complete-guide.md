## What’s new and why it matters
ORA-12225: TNS: Destination Host Unreachable — Causes, Fixes & Prevention ORA-12225 is an Oracle Net (TNS) error that occurs when the Oracle client cannot establish a network-level connection to the target database host. Unlike a simple listener failure, this error indicates that TCP/IP packets cannot even reach the destination server.
