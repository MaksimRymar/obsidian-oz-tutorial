## What’s new and why it matters
Welcome to Day 5! Special methods (also known as magic methods or dunder methods , short for "double underscore") allow your custom Python classes to hook into Python's built-in operators, syntax, and runtime protocols. By implementing dunder methods, you make your custom objects act like built-in Python types ( list , dict , int ), enabling features like pretty printing, iteration, comparison, context management, and direct execution. 1.
