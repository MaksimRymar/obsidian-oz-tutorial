## What’s new and why it matters
Most AI agents die when you close the chat. Mine don't. They're called sparks , and they live in Flash , my open source agent shell for Ollama . Each one gets a name, a goal, a schedule, and lines it must never cross. Then it works on its own, on your machine, and reports back. Make a spark that checks my repo every morning for new issues and tells me which ones look like bugs. Never comment on anything. Two sparks were fine. Six were six interns who had never met.
