I used the UCI Wine Quality files to make that gap visible with two small regressors, one trained on red wine records and one on white. The target is the sensory quality score from the source. This article records the saved run from November 4, 2026; publishing the article adds no new training. The source files contain 1,599 red rows and 4,898 white rows, 6,497 in total.
