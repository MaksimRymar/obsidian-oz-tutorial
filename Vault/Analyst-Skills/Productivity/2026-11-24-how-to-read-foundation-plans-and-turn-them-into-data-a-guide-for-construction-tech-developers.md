## What’s new and why it matters
If you're building software for construction (estimating tools, takeoff automation, BIM integrations, or AI that reads drawings), you'll quickly run into a problem: the data you need is locked inside blueprints. And the first sheet that matters is usually the foundation plan. A foundation plan is a top-down drawing of a building's base. It shows footings, foundation walls, piers, columns, and slabs, with their sizes, locations, and reinforcement. Contractors use it to know what to dig and pour.
