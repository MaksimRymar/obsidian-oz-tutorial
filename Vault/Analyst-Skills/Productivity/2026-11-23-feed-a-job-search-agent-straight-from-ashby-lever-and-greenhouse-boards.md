## What’s new and why it matters
A job-search agent that reads aggregator pages works with copies. Copies can lag behind the original posting, list one role several times and drop the salary. For many tech companies the original lives in one of three applicant tracking systems: Ashby, Lever or Greenhouse. Each of them offers companies a public job board, and each board has a public API. I built three small Actors that read those public boards and return one row per open job: Ashby Jobs Feed , Lever Jobs Feed and Greenhouse Jobs Feed .
