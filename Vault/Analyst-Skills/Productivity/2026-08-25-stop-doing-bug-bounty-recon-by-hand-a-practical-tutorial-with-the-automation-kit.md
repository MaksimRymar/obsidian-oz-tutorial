## What’s new and why it matters
If you hunt bug bounty targets for more than a week, you'll notice a depressing pattern: the actual hunting is maybe 20% of your time. The other 80% is recon — enumerating subdomains, resolving DNS, probing HTTP, diffing what changed since yesterday. It's tedious, mechanical work, and it's exactly the kind of work a machine should do while you sleep. This is a step-by-step tutorial on turning your recon into an automated pipeline using the Bug Bounty Automation Kit ($15 on LemonSqueezy) .
