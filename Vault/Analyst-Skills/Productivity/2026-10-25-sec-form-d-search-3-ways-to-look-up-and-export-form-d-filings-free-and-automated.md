## What’s new and why it matters
If you want a list of US companies that just raised private money, the place to look is Form D . It's the short notice a company files with the SEC after it sells shares in a private round. It's public and free to read. The hard part is searching and exporting it. Here are three ways to do it, from free and manual to fully automated.
