## What’s new and why it matters
Challenge.gov, the federal portal for prize competitions, was retired on March 30, 2026. Its archive moved to GSA's challenges-and-prizes repository on GitHub. Active challenges are now ordinary web pages on USA.gov, one page per challenge, with no API, no RSS and no export, and some agencies post challenges only on their own sites. The old community scraper read challenge.gov's home page, so it stopped working the same day.
