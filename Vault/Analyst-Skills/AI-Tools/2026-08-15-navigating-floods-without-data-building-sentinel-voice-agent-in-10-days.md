## What’s new and why it matters
It was during the peak monsoon season when I read a distress report from a family stranded on their rooftop. Power was flickering, rain was hammering against the walls, and cellular data was down to a crawling 2G edge. They had a phone with 14% battery, but opening an emergency app or downloading heavy government disaster PDFs was impossible. All they could do was place a direct phone call. That moment stayed with me. When panic sets in and water is rising inside your living room, you don't navigate drop-down menus or type search queries into a browser. You need to speak, and you need a voice…
