## What’s new and why it matters
I thought open-sourcing my project would get me stars. Instead, strangers broke it. And honestly, that was much more valuable. Two months ago, I started building BOOTH , a small checkpoint layer that sits between an LLM call and your application and decides whether the response is trustworthy enough to use. It's intentionally small. Zero dependencies. Just mine. For Hacktoberfest, I opened one issue: Test BOOTH against your favorite LLM provider. Document what breaks. I expected silence. Instead, people started finding things I hadn't seen. A 7B model reported confidence: 1.0 on wrong answers…
