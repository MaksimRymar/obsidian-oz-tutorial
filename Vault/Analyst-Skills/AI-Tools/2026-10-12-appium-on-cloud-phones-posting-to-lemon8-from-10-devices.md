## What’s new and why it matters
Lemon8 has no public posting API in October 2026. Every post goes through the mobile app: tap the plus button, pick photos from the gallery, choose a template, type a headline and a description, put the hashtags at the end, publish. For one account that takes two minutes. For ten accounts every day, somebody on your team loses a good part of each morning tapping through the same five screens. Appium can do the tapping.
