## What’s new and why it matters
Short videos are where a lot of today's marketing, product feedback and trends live, but the words are locked inside the video. TikTok and Instagram don't give you the transcript, and most videos have no captions. The usual do-it-yourself route has three parts: download the video (TikTok and Instagram block many server IPs), extract the audio, and run a speech-to-text model. In this post I'll do all three with one API call from Python, and then go one step further: transcribe a whole TikTok account and rank its videos by views, with the first sentence of each one (the "hook").
