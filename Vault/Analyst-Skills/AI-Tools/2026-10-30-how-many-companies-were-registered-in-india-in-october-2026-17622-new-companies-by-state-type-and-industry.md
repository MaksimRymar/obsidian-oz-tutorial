## What’s new and why it matters
India's Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) registers new companies every working day, but its own portal answers one company at a time, behind a captcha. There is no public "companies incorporated this week" list you can download. I pulled every company the MCA register lists as incorporated from 1 to 29 October 2026: 17,622 companies across 26 register dates , and grouped them by state, company type and industry.
