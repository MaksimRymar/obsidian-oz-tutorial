## What’s new and why it matters
Generated documentation stays trustworthy when every heading has an explicit owner lane before prose is written. Extractable facts can be compiled from source, schemas, and fixtures without inventing any binding product promises. Draft glue may summarize those facts, yet signed claims about support, security, and pricing still require a human owner. A small provenance checker can block merges when unsigned binding language appears under the wrong heading.
