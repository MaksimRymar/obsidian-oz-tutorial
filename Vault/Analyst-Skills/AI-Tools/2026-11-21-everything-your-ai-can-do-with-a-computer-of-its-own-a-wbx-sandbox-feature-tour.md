## What’s new and why it matters
Isolated Linux microVMs in the EU, for AI assistants without code and for agents you build yourself. AI assistants are good at writing code. Running it is a different story. The assistant can't install packages on your computer, it shouldn't touch your files, and a chat window is a poor place for a two-hour job. WBX Sandbox gives the AI its own isolated computer in the EU. It installs what it needs, runs the code, and hands back the result: a file, a chart, a link, a test report. Nothing runs on your machine. This post is a tour of what the platform does, so you can picture what to build with…
