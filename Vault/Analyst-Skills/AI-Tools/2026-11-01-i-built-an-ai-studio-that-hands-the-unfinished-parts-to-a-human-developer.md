## What’s new and why it matters
Most AI app builders end at the same place: you get a demo that looks great, then you open the code and realize someone still has to wire up payments, a database, email, auth. The tool pretends that part doesn't exist. I wanted the opposite. A studio that builds as much as a browser can run, checks that it actually works, and then writes down honestly what is left for a human. This post is about how that works inside, the bugs that bit me, and what it costs per project. Live: aiuniversenetwork.com (there's a no-signup demo on the landing page).
