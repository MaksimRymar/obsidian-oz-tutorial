## What’s new and why it matters
You will build a receipt sorter: drop receipt photos into a web page and get a CSV with the company, date and total of each one. Then you will use AcruxCore ( an LLMops platform ) to choose the model with evidence instead of a guess. You finish with a versioned image prompt, a 50-receipt test set, a model comparison you can repeat, and a trace for every receipt the app reads. Every AcruxCore step shows the dashboard and the Python SDK side by side. The full code is in github.com/talhaanwarch/receipt-sorter .
