## What’s new and why it matters
Most "AI agent" demos stop at the moment it gets interesting for a company: who's allowed to let the agent pay an invoice, send an email or change a record, and how do you know what it did? That's the problem I've been working on with Agent Hangar , an open-source (Apache-2.0), self-hosted platform where agents work as digital employees . It covers the whole range: from a simple chat agent, to agents built in other frameworks, all the way to a Digital employee with a harness (Claude Code, Codex) that works in your IDE with its own role, budget and authority.
