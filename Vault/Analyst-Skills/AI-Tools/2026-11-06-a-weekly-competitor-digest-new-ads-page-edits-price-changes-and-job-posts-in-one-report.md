## What’s new and why it matters
Keeping an eye on competitors usually means five tabs: the Meta Ad Library, the TikTok Ad Library, their pricing page, their store and their careers page. Each one is easy to check once and easy to forget the week after. What you actually want is a short note per competitor that says what is new, and silence when nothing is. This post sets that up as a scheduled job, then compares the competitor monitors on the Apify Store using the prices their Store pages listed at the time of writing.
