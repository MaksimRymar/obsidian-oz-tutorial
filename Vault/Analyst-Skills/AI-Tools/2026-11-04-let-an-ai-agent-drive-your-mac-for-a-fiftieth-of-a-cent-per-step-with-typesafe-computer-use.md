## What’s new and why it matters
Computer-use agents built on frontier models work, but every step ships a screenshot to a big model and waits several seconds for a plan. A twelve-step task can cost close to a dollar. Most of those steps do not need a plan at all: they need one choice from a short list of buttons. awlevin/typesafe-computer-use , called jev for short, is built on that observation. Per the README it drives a Mac toward a goal you type in plain English for about a fiftieth of a cent per step.
