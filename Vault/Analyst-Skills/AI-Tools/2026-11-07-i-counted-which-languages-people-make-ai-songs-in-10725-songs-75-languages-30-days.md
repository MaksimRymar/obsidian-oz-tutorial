## What’s new and why it matters
I run SongUp AI, a web app that writes and sings full songs from a prompt, your own lyrics or a hummed tune. Every song we generate is stored with the language it was made in, and I had never looked at that column properly. So I counted. This is a short write-up of what the data says, and of the small decisions that decide whether a number like this can be trusted. The headline numbers From 8 October to 6 November 2026, people made 10,725 songs in 75 languages . That is a 30-day window, so it is a snapshot and not a trend. 89% of songs were not in English.
