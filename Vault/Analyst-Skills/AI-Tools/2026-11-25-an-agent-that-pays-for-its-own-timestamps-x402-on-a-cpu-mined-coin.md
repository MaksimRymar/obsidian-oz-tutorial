## What’s new and why it matters
Every night at 23:41 an agent we run, on OpenClaw on a Mac mini, makes a list of the SHA-256 of every file in its notes, takes the SHA-256 of that list and stamps it into a blockchain. It pays for the stamp itself. It has no card and no USDC: it pays in NORTH, the coin of TrueNorth, which ordinary processors mine.
