## What’s new and why it matters
This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge: Week 3 . What I Built Modo Tortuga ("Turtle Mode") is for the days when the list is so long that the laziest option is to not start at all. You give it a plain text file with up to 30 to-dos, one per line, written however you like. A Gemma model running on your laptop picks at most three and gives you a first step for each: something you can do in 15 minutes or less that leaves a small, visible result. Then it stops. It doesn't reschedule anything, send anything, or touch your calendar. It just points. The effort it saves is…
