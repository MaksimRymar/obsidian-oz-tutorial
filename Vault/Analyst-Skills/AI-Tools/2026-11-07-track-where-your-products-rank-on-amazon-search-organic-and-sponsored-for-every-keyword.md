## What’s new and why it matters
For an Amazon seller, the search results page is the shop window. Where your product sits for "coffee grinder", whether it is there organically or only because you pay for the ad slot, and who sits above you at what price: that decides most sales. Seller tools show some of it, for a monthly fee. This post builds a small rank tracker for your own keyword list, then compares the Amazon search scrapers on the Apify Store using the prices their Store pages listed at the time of writing.
