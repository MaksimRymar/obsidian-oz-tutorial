## What’s new and why it matters
Many household money leaks are small and quiet. A washing machine breaks a few months before its guarantee runs out, and the repair gets paid without anyone asking the seller. A free trial turns into a monthly charge. A card payment has no receipt by the time someone needs one. None of this is hard to spot. It is just easy to miss. An agent sounds like a good fit: read the records, point at what needs a decision.
