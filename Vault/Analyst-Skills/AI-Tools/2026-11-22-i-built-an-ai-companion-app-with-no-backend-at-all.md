## What’s new and why it matters
Every AI companion app I tried wanted the same three things: a monthly subscription, an account, and my conversations living on their server forever. The "memory" was always a black box too — the character would forget something important, and there was no way to see what it remembered, let alone fix it. So I built the opposite. Xinyu is an AI companion app for Android with no backend. Not "backend I promise to keep private" — there is literally no server.
