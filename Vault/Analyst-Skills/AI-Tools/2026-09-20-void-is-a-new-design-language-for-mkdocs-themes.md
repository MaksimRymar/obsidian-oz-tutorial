## What’s new and why it matters
Void is a new design language for MkDocs themes: a drop-in theme (name: void) and companion plugin on top of MkDocs. Void is a custom MkDocs theme that blends the translucent, layered aesthetics of Glass design system with the minimal, industrial clarity of NothingOS. It combines pure black canvas, glass morphism panels, dot-matrix typography, and Nothing Red accents into a cohesive documentation experience.
