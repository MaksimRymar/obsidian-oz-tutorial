## What’s new and why it matters
The federal WARN Act asks many employers with 100 or more employees to give at least 60 calendar days of written notice before a plant closing or mass layoff ( U.S. Department of Labor ). Each state publishes the notices it receives. I pulled them from 19 state agencies, put them in one table, and looked at the gap between the notice date and the layoff date. What I used 6,459 notices posted between Oct 2024 and Oct 2026, from state labor agencies. Of those, 5,510 publish both a notice date and an effective (layoff or closure) date as a calendar day.
