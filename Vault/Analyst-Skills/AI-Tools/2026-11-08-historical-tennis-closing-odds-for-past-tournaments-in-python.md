A tennis model or a betting system is only as good as the backtest behind it, and a backtest needs the market's closing prices for the matches you test on. Daily feeds give you that from now on; for last year's Wimbledon or the 2023 Paris Masters you need the archive. BetExplorer keeps one public results page per tournament season with the closing odds of every match, and each match page lists the closing price of every bookmaker it tracked.
