## What’s new and why it matters
A CAS Registry Number (CAS RN) is a unique numeric identifier assigned by the Chemical Abstracts Service to a chemical substance. Unlike a trivial name, it does not change when people use a different synonym, which makes it the most practical key for matching substances across safety data sheets (SDS), supplier catalogues and datasets. Format A CAS number has three parts separated by hyphens: XXXXXXX-YY-Z . the first part has 2 to 7 digits, the second part has exactly 2 digits, the last part is a single check digit.
