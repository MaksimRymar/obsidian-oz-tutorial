## What’s new and why it matters
An enterprise AI frameworks comparison usually ranks tools by features or raw speed, and that is why the results mislead. Speed barely separates the leading options, because the model call dominates each run. In one recent benchmark, six mainstream frameworks averaged between 6.79 and 8.39 seconds per run. What separates them is behavior under failure: repeated steps, wasted tokens and lost state. This guide defines four measures that expose those gaps, checks five frameworks against them, and ends with a complete Python scorecard that you can run on your own workload data.
