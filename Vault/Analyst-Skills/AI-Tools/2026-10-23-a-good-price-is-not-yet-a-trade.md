## What’s new and why it matters
When I look at the result of a trading bot, knowing how much it earned is not enough for me. I also want to understand a more down-to-earth thing: at what price did it actually buy and sell? The order asked for one price. Did the exchange give the same price, a better one, or a worse one? I checked this against backup copies of the databases of two of our live bots. I took the available history from 29 August to 22 October 2026. For this review I kept only limit orders — the ones that state a price boundary we agree to buy or sell at.
