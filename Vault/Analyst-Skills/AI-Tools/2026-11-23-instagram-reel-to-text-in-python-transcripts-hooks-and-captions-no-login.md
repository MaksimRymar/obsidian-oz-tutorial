## What’s new and why it matters
Content marketers, agencies and creators study reels that work: what is said in the first seconds, how the script is built, which call to action closes it. Watching reels and typing out what they say does not scale past a few, and the caption rarely holds the script. This post turns Instagram reel links into transcripts in Python, lines up the hooks of the most-liked reels, and sets up a daily run that transcribes only reels it has not seen before.
