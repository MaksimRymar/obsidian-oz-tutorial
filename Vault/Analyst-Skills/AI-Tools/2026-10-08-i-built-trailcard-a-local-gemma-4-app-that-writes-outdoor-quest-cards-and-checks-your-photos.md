## What’s new and why it matters
This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 1: Touch Grass What I Built Trailcard is a local-first web app that uses Gemma 4 (running on your own machine via Ollama) to write a printable one-page outdoor quest card — then checks your photos when you get back. The pitch in one sentence: the screen is the shortest part . You open the app, describe where you're headed and how long you have, and a small open-weight model writes a card of observation tasks tailored to your season, habitat and energy level.
