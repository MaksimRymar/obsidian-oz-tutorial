## What’s new and why it matters
YouTube is one of the best knowledge sources you can put behind an LLM: conference talks, tutorials, product demos, earnings calls, podcasts. The problem is getting the text out. The official route doesn't work for this. The YouTube Data API's captions.download endpoint needs OAuth and only works for videos you own (or where the owner allows third-party contributions). For "index this competitor's channel" or "build a RAG bot over 300 conference talks", it's a dead end. The usual Python workaround libraries break whenever YouTube changes its internals and get your server IP blocked after a few…
