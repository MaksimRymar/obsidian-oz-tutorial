## What’s new and why it matters
Sellers who move beyond amazon.com hit the same question on every new marketplace: what sells for this keyword in Germany, Japan, the UK or India, at what price, and who advertises on it. Amazon also treats a foreign visitor differently: from a US address, amazon.co.uk drops VAT from its prices or hides them, and amazon.co.jp shows a different set of products. Research done from the wrong country gives the wrong answer. This post reads Amazon search results from the four marketplaces into Python, in local currency, as a local shopper sees them.
