## What’s new and why it matters
What Is Text-to-SQL? Text-to-SQL is a technology that translates natural-language questions into executable SQL queries, enabling non-technical users to query relational databases without writing code. Modern text-to-SQL systems achieve 85–92% accuracy on enterprise schema benchmarks — a dramatic improvement from 2023 levels — by combining large language models with schema-aware prompting, few-shot examples, and semantic-layer grounding.
