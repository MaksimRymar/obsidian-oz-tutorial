## What’s new and why it matters
Resellers and collectors on Vinted all fight the same clock: a good listing at the right price is gone within minutes, and refreshing a dozen saved searches by hand on several country sites does not scale. What you want is a feed: every new listing for your searches, with price, brand, size and condition, as soon as it shows up. This post reads Vinted search results into Python from the same search links you build in your browser, compares asking prices across country sites, and sets up a schedule that sends only the new listings to Slack.
