## What’s new and why it matters
This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 2: Side Hustle What I Built CreatorDesk is a self-hosted, open-source tool that solves one specific problem for independent digital artists: tracking which version of a commission a client approved, and why. An artist uploads a work-in-progress (a sketch, a lineart, a colored render), sends the client a private link, and the client clicks Approve or Request changes with optional written feedback.
