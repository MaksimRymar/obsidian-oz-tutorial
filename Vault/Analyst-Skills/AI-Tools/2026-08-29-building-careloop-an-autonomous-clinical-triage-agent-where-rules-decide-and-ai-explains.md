## What’s new and why it matters
I created this content for the purposes of entering the All Things Agentic Hackathon. The problem that started it A doctor gets about eight minutes with a patient and, for anyone with a real history, forty pages of scattered records — lab reports, discharge notes, and pharmacy bills from three different clinics. So the history is effectively invisible at the exact moment it matters most. And when the visit ends, nothing follows up: the six-month course lapses at week five, the recheck never gets booked. I wanted to build an agent that closes that loop — one that reads the mess, decides urgency…
