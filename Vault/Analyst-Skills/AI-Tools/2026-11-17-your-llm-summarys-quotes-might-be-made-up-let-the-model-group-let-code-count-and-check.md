## What’s new and why it matters
Give a model a few hundred reviews and ask "what do people praise and complain about?" and in a minute you get a report that reads well. It has two defects that are easy to miss, because both still look right: The quotes aren't quotes. Models like to tidy a comment up and then put quote marks around the tidy version.
