## What’s new and why it matters
A few days ago, I opened BOOTH for Hacktoberfest contributors. BOOTH is a lightweight, provider-agnostic Python library that adds a checkpoint before an LLM output reaches your application. check() / acheck() handle ambiguity and confidence checks, while check_with_evidence() lets you compare an answer against evidence retrieved by your RAG pipeline. Since Hacktoberfest started, developers have begun contributing integrations across different LLM providers and models.
