## What’s new and why it matters
This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 3: Slow and Steady What I Built Owe pastes the message you have been avoiding. Gemma 4 ( gemma-4-26b-a4b-it , via the Gemini API) drafts a reply of at most 60 words, names the one fact it must not invent, and says whether you can send it. owe/guard.py then runs locally and can override that answer. A number, email, or name that was not in the message keeps the reply unsent. The message is sent to the Gemini API. This does not run offline. The send decision stays on the machine.
