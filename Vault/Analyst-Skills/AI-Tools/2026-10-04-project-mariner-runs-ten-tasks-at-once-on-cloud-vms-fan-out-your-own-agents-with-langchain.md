## What’s new and why it matters
What Project Mariner does Project Mariner is Google's agent that browses and acts on websites for people. At I/O in May 2025, Google said the new version runs on virtual machines in the cloud. That lets it handle up to 10 tasks at once while people get on with other work. It launched first for U.S. subscribers to the $249.99-a-month AI Ultra plan. ( TechCrunch ) The pattern When the agent runs in the user's own browser, it does one thing at a time, and it stops when the laptop closes. Moving it to cloud machines means one machine per task .
