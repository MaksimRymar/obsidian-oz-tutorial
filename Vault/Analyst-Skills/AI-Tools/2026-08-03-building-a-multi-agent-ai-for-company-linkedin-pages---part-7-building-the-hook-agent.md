## What’s new and why it matters
Our system can now classify a topic, conduct research, back it with examples, identify weak assumptions and unsupported claims, and structure everything into a content brief before handing it over to the writing agents. Before the writing agents generate a full post, we need an opening that's compelling enough to stop people from scrolling. That's why we built the Hook Agent. Unlike the previous agents, it doesn't consume the topic, research, examples or critique directly. Instead, it only takes the ContentBrief generated by the Brief Agent.
