## What’s new and why it matters
I keep my notes in markdown. Hundreds of files: RFC summaries, postmortems, the bits of HTTP caching I have to re-learn every time a CDN misbehaves. Writing them is easy. Remembering them is not. Two months later I'm searching my own notes and still getting no-cache and no-store mixed up. Flashcards fix the remembering part, but making them by hand takes so long that I never kept it up. Asking an LLM to make them is fast, but it's risky. Sometimes the model invents an answer that sounds right and isn't in the notes, and then you memorize something false.
