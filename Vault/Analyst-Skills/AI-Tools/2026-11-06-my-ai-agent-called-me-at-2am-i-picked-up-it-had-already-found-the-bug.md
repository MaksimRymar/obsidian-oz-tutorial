## What’s new and why it matters

Roll back or hotfix? I hit the green button. Maya spoke first: "Hey. The payments test started failing after the last merge. I already checked: it's the currency rounding change. I can roll it back now, or write the fix. Which one?" I said "roll it back". She said "on it", hung up, and posted a three-line recap in our chat. That was the whole incident. Forty seconds.
