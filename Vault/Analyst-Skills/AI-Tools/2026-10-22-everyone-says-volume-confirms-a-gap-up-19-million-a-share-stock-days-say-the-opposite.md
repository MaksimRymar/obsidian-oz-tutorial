## What’s new and why it matters
A gap up on heavy volume is supposed to be confirmation . A gap up on thin volume is supposed to be a trap. I finally had the data to check it: 641 adjusted daily bars for 3,116 Shanghai/Shenzhen main-board stocks — 1.91 million stock-days , running through the 2026-10-22 close, pulled from a free public quote endpoint. No vendor, no API key, no paid feed. The folklore is backwards. Holding gap size fixed, the below-average-volume gap beat the 3x-volume gap in every bucket I measured, on both the next day and five days out. And the bucket most retail traders chase — opening at the board limit…
