## What’s new and why it matters
Ask a dentist where they rank on Google Maps for "dentist" and they will search from their own chair and say "first". Two kilometres away, a customer searching the same word sees a different list, and the clinic may be eighth, or missing. Google Maps ranks local businesses partly by where the searcher's map is, so a single rank number is a guess. The useful answer is a grid: the same search run from many points around the business, with the rank at each one.
