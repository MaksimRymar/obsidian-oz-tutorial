Your Training Data Has a Geography Problem We talk a lot about dataset bias — demographic, temporal, linguistic. Here's one that barely gets discussed: where your data was collected from. The web serves different content depending on the visitor's IP location. Prices, search results, ads, product catalogs, even page layouts vary by country. A dataset assembled from a single vantage point inherits that one vantage point's worldview — and because everything looks normal from inside it, the bias is invisible until your model meets the real world.
