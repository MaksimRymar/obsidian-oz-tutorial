## What’s new and why it matters
Editing one real estate photo (bracket merge, straight verticals, bright windows) costs from $0.25 to $5 depending on who does it. We read the price pages of 27 photo editing services and apps made for listing photos in November 2026 and put every row in an open dataset ( real-estate-photo-editing-prices.csv , CC BY 4.0). What we found $0.90 : median price for one edited HDR photo across the 18 providers that publish a per-photo price. $0.99 with human editors (11 services), $0.80 with AI tools (5 apps).
