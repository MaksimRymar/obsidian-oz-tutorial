## What’s new and why it matters
Shipments are the number brands see first, and in the weeks after a 7-Eleven (7-11) listing they usually look great. Distribution is being filled and stores are taking their opening stock. If you read only that number, you can spend a month believing a product is selling when shoppers have barely started. The fix is simple to describe: put what was shipped in (sell-in) next to what shoppers bought (sell-out), and derive how many weeks of stock are sitting in the system.
