## What’s new and why it matters
I built this for Hacktoberfest Week 3 ("Slow and Steady") but missed the deadline, so this is a regular post and not a challenge entry. What I Built Lazy Cook is a small web app for tired people who stand at the fridge and cannot decide what to cook. You type what you have, how many minutes you can spare, your diet, and what you can cook with (one pan on gas, a pressure cooker, or a microwave). It gives you one easy Indian dish, a shopping list of 3 items at most, and 3 to 6 steps that fit your time.
