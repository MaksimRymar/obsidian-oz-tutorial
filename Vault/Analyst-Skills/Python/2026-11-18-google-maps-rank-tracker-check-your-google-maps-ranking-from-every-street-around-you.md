## What’s new and why it matters
"Where do we rank on Google Maps?" has no single answer. Google Maps orders local results partly by where the searcher's map is, so a clinic can be first for "dentist" on its own block and eighth, or missing, a few kilometres away. Searching from your own desk only tells you about your desk. A Google Maps rank tracker answers it properly: it runs the same search from a grid of points around the business and records the rank at each one.
