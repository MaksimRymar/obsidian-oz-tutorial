## What’s new and why it matters
My job discovery pipeline ( remote-job-engine ) sweeps dozens of ATS boards and aggregators every day. Until recently its output was a CSV I opened in a text editor, which is fine when you are hunting for one job and useless when you want to see the shape of the whole market. So on 17 November I froze one sweep: 15,516 live postings from 73 sources, covering 1,638 distinct companies and board slugs.
