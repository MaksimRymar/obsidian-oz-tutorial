## What’s new and why it matters
The move data engineer to data architect is the single most misunderstood step on the data career ladder, because it looks like a promotion inside the same job when it is actually a change of job entirely — the day you become an architect you stop being measured on the pipelines you ship and start being measured on the decisions other people ship because of you. A strong data engineer is graded on throughput: DAGs that run green, latency that stays under SLA, tables that land on time, bugs that get fixed fast.
