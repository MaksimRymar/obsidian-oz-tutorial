## What’s new and why it matters
Most "Physics formula" websites are the same thing: a giant wall of equations with zero context. You find the formula, but you still don't know why it works, when to use it, or where people usually mess it up. That frustration is basically the origin story of Formulaverse — a site I built solo, for JEE/NEET aspirants (and honestly, anyone who's curious about physics), where every formula comes with the explanation it deserves instead of just... existing.
