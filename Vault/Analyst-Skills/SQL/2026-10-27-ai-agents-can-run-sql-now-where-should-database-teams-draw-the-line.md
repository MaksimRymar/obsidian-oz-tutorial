## What’s new and why it matters
For most developers, AI in database work has meant one thing so far: typing less. You describe what you need, a model drafts the SQL and you decide what to keep. The tool got smarter every year, but the shape of the job stayed the same. You were still the one who pressed Run. Agentic AI changes the shape of the job. An agent can inspect a schema, execute a query, read the result and decide what to do next on its own. The question in front of database teams is no longer whether AI can write good SQL.
