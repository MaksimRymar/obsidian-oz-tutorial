## What’s new and why it matters

The SQL is familiar, but the editor, schema browser, result grid, shortcuts, and packaging expectations keep changing. Even small differences accumulate when you move between PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, and Oracle throughout the week. I built omni-sql to explore a narrower idea: one focused desktop SQL workspace that behaves consistently across the major relational databases, without trying to become a complete administration suite.
