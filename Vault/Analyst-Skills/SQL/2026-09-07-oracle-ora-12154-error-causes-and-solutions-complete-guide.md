## What’s new and why it matters
ORA-12154: TNS Could Not Resolve the Connect Identifier Specified ORA-12154 is one of the most common Oracle connectivity errors, occurring when the Oracle client cannot find or resolve the TNS alias (connect identifier) you specified in your connection string. This typically means Oracle cannot locate the alias in tnsnames.ora , cannot find the file itself, or the naming method is misconfigured.
