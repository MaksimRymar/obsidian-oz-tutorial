## What’s new and why it matters
I taught myself SQL. No bootcamp and no data degree. I took an online course, did a lot of practice problems, and then started answering real questions at work. If you’re in sales ops, RevOps, or finance and thinking about learning it, here is what I’d pass on. Start with questions, not syntax Pick three questions you get asked every month. Which reps are creating pipeline? Where do deals stall? Which accounts have gone quiet? Learn only what you need to answer those.
