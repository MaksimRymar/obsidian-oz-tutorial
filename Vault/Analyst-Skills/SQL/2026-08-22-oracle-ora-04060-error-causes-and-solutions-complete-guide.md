## What’s new and why it matters
ORA-04060: Insufficient Privileges to Execute — Causes, Fixes & Prevention ORA-04060 is an Oracle database error that occurs when a user attempts to execute a stored procedure, function, package, or trigger without the necessary EXECUTE privilege. Unlike general privilege errors (ORA-01031), this error is specifically tied to PL/SQL object execution rights. It is one of the most common errors encountered after deployments, often causing application outages that require immediate DBA intervention.
