## What’s new and why it matters
Nakodo finds creators and local businesses for a brand by searching platforms for keywords. The keywords come from a research pass over the brand's website: a brief, a set of target markets, and a list of search phrases grouped by angle. That list is finite. Every plan caps it, and the caps are published on the pricing page : 8 search keywords per campaign on Free, 25 on Pro, 60 on Business. So a campaign that runs for long enough reaches a state where every keyword it has has been searched, on every platform, in every market. The searching stops.
