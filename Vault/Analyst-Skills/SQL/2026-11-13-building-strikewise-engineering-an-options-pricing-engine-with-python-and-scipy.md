## What’s new and why it matters
Options trading involves more than predicting whether an underlying index will move up or down. A change in the underlying price, implied volatility, interest rates, and time to expiry can all affect an option's theoretical premium. I wanted to build a project that explored these relationships mathematically while also applying practical software engineering concepts. That led me to build STRIKEWISE, an open-source quantitative options pricing and risk engine focused on NIFTY 50 options.
