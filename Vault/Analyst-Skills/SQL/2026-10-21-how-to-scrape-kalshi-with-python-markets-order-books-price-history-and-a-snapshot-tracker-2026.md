## What’s new and why it matters
Kalshi has become one of the more interesting public datasets around. Every market is a running, money-backed estimate of how likely something is: an election, a Fed decision, tomorrow's high temperature in New York. If you do research, build dashboards, or want to compare Kalshi against polls or other prediction markets, you'll want that data in your own database. The good news is that you don't need to scrape HTML at all. The Kalshi API serves market data publicly, with no key and no login.
