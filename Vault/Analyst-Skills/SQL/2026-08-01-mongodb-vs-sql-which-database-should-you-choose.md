## What’s new and why it matters
Choosing a database is one of the most consequential decisions you'll make on a project. It shapes how you model data, write queries, scale your system, and maintain consistency and getting it wrong doesn't usually hurt on day one. It hurts eighteen months in, when the schema is load-bearing and migrating away is expensive. Two of the most common options developers weigh against each other are MongoDB, a document-oriented NoSQL database, and SQL databases like PostgreSQL, MySQL, and SQL Server.
