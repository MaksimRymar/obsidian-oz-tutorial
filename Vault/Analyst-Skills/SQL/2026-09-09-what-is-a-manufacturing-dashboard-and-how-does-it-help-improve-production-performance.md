## What’s new and why it matters
Manufacturing performance can change quickly. A machine slowdown, material shortage, quality issue, or production delay can affect output before the problem becomes obvious in a traditional report. That is why manufacturers need more than disconnected spreadsheets and end-of-day reports. They need a clear way to understand what is happening across production while there is still time to act. A manufacturing dashboard brings critical production data such as output, downtime, OEE, machine utilization, quality, capacity, inventory, and production costs into one visual view.
