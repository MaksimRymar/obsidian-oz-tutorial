## What’s new and why it matters
I counted 1,726 job postings as "other". My classifier was lying, and it moved a headline number. I publish a free dataset of job postings where the employer states a pay range. To make it more than a CSV, I group every posting into a role family — engineering, sales, product, design, support, and so on — so readers can ask "what does this company pay a data scientist" instead of "what is the average of everything". Last week the distribution looked wrong in a way that was easy to wave away: family postings share engineering 3,188 39.7% other 1,726 21.5% sales 868 10.8% support / ops 510 6.3%…
