## What’s new and why it matters
I work in education software, and for years the same question landed on my desk. A junior developer, standing next to my chair or dropping a message: "Okay, I need a student's name and their latest grade. Where do I even start?" And every time, I realized I wasn't answering their real question. Their real question was never which table holds the student's name — students.name , easy. The question was: which tables do I have to pass through? Because that's how an education database actually feels once you're inside one. You're standing at grades and you need to get to students . Straight line?…
