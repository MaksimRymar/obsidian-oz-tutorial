## What’s new and why it matters
If you've ever bought a phone or laptop in Bangladesh, you know the routine: open Star Tech, Ryans, Pickaboo, Apple Gadgets and a few Facebook-page shops in separate tabs, then compare prices by hand. The same model can differ by several thousand taka between shops, and prices change every week. I got tired of that, so I built KomKoto.bd (কম কত — "how much less?"). It shows the listed price of one product across Bangladeshi retailers on a single page. What it does Compares prices across 22 shops.
