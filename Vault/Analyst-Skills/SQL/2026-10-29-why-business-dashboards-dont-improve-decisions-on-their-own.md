## What’s new and why it matters
Businesses can collect enormous amounts of data and still struggle to make timely decisions. Teams track revenue, delivery performance, and key performance indicators (KPIs), yet important questions remain: What changed, why did it happen, and who should respond? The problem is often the gap between seeing information and taking action . Inconsistent KPIs, stale reports, missing alerts, unclear ownership, and disconnected workflows can prevent dashboards from improving operations.
