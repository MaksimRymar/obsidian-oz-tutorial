## What’s new and why it matters
Picture opening Sunrise Supermarket. Before you sell a single item, you build the shelves tables for customers , products , orders . That's construction. Once the shelves are up, you run the shop taking orders, updating them, clearing out the cancelled ones. In SQL, these are two different jobs: DDL and DML . What is DDL? DDL stands for Data Definition Language. These are the commands that build and shape your database creating tables, changing their structure, or removing them entirely.
