## What’s new and why it matters
I'm a solo developer, and I built VigieWeb , a desktop SEO audit app for Windows aimed at freelancers and small agencies. It's written in Python with Qt (PySide6). Why a desktop app? Most SEO tools are monthly subscriptions that live in the cloud. For a freelancer who audits a handful of client sites, that's a recurring cost for an occasional need. I wanted a tool you buy once and run locally.
