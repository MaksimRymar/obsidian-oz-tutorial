## What’s new and why it matters
A TikTok Shop campaign can produce a strong ROAS and still take too long to pay back its customer acquisition cost. Campaign ROAS combines new and returning buyers, mixes product margins, and often stops at revenue. A cohort view asks a narrower question: after all costs attached to the buyers acquired in one period, when did the business recover what it spent to acquire them?
