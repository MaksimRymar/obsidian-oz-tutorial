## What’s new and why it matters
ORA-12012: Error on Auto Execute of Job — Causes, Fixes & Prevention What Is ORA-12012? ORA-12012 is a wrapper error raised by Oracle's job execution engine (DBMS_JOB or DBMS_SCHEDULER) when an automatically scheduled job fails during execution. It rarely appears alone — you'll almost always see it accompanied by secondary errors like ORA-06512, ORA-01031, or ORA-01555 in the alert.log or trace files, which reveal the true root cause.
