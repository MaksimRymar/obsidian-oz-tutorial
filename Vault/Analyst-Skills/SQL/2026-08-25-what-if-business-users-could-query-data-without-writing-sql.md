## What’s new and why it matters
Most companies don’t have a data shortage. They have an answer-access problem . A simple question like “Which regions saw the biggest drop in conversions this month?” can still require someone to raise a BI ticket, wait for an analyst, get SQL written, validate the result, and repeat the process for every follow-up question.
