## What’s new and why it matters
ORA-12170: TNS Connect Timeout Occurred — Causes, Fixes & Prevention ORA-12170 is an Oracle Net (TNS) error that occurs when a client fails to establish a connection to the database within the configured timeout period. The error indicates that the listener or server process did not respond in time, causing the TNS layer to abort the connection attempt.
