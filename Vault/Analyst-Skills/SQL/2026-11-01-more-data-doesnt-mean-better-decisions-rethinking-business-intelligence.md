## What’s new and why it matters
A company can collect enormous amounts of information, build beautiful dashboards, and still make terrible decisions. Why? Business Intelligence is not about having more data. It is about reducing the distance between what an organization knows and what it can intelligently do. A supermarket records every sale. Its online store records purchases too. Loyalty accounts can connect some of those purchases to customers, while inventory systems keep track of what is left on the shelves.
