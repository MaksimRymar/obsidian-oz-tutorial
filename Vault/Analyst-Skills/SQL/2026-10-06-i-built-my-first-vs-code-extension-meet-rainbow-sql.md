## What’s new and why it matters
I have a confession to make. For years, every time I had to write or edit a SQL INSERT statement, I did the same little dance: put my cursor on a value, count the commas, scroll back up to the column list, count again, and hope I landed on the right spot. Then I'd change the value... and still not be 100% sure it was the right column. With three or four columns, fine. But give me a table with fifteen columns and a few rows of data, and I'm suddenly back in school, counting on my fingers.
