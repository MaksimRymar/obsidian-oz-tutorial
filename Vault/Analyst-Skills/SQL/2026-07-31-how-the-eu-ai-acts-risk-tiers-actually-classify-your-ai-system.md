## What’s new and why it matters
The EU AI Act isn't just incoming regulation - it classifies AI systems by risk and sorts what you're required to build, document, and prove before you ship. The Risk Tier System and What It Requires The Act sorts AI systems into four risk tiers: unacceptable risk (banned outright), high risk, limited risk, and minimal risk. Most teams building internal tools, chatbots, or recommendation features land in the limited or minimal risk buckets - which carry light obligations, mostly around transparency (telling users they're interacting with AI).
