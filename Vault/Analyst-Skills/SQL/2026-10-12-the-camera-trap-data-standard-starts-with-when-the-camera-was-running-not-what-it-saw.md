## What’s new and why it matters
Three raccoon visits in a week reads like a fact about raccoons. It is at least as much a fact about the camera: how many of those nights it was running, how far out it could make out movement, and whether the clock stamping each clip was right. Wildlife researchers who leave cameras out unattended share a data format that makes them write those things down, and it gives them a table of their own, ahead of anything the camera saw.
