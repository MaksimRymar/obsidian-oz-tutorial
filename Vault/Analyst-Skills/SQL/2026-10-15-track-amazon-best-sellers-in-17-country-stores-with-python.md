## What’s new and why it matters
If you're monitoring e-commerce trends, running a brand, or feeding a shopping assistant, you probably want what's selling on Amazon right now — not next week, and not just in the US. Amazon publishes the Best Sellers and Hot New Releases charts for every category, in 17 country stores , updated every hour. Scraping those pages is cheap and doesn't need a login — this post shows how to do it from Python in three ways: today's top sellers, a cross-marketplace comparison, and a daily tracker that logs rank + price changes into SQLite.
