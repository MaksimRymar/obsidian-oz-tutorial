Ashby is the hiring system many AI companies and venture-backed startups moved to in the last few years: OpenAI, Snowflake, Harvey, Airwallex, Applied Intuition, Crusoe, Mistral, Legora. Like Greenhouse, every Ashby job board has a public JSON API. We read all the boards we know of every night, so we can compare the two. Numbers below are from the November 18, 2026 build: 50,121 open jobs on 2,180 Ashby boards and 164,004 on 3,976 Greenhouse boards .
