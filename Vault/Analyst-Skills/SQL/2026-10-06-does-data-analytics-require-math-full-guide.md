## What’s new and why it matters
Yes, data analytics requires some math, but you do not need to be an advanced mathematician to become a data analyst. Most data analyst can start with basic mathematical concepts and gradually learn the statistical techniques required for analyzing data. In practical data analytics, tools such as Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Python perform many calculations for you. The more important skill is understanding what the numbers mean, choosing the right method, and using the results to answer business questions.
