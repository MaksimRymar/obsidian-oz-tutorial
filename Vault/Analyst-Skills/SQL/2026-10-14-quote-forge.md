## What’s new and why it matters
This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 2: Side Hustle What I Built I built QuoteForge Local , a private, local-first AI proposal and quote builder designed for freelancers. A freelancer can enter: A client's project brief Service or project type Hourly rate Estimated hours Additional expenses QuoteForge then generates a professional proposal draft using the open-weight Llama 3.2 model running locally through Ollama .
