## What’s new and why it matters
Data products are what you get when you stop treating a dataset as a byproduct of a pipeline and start treating it as something you ship — an owned, versioned, governed unit of data that another team can find, understand, trust, and consume without ever messaging you on Slack to ask "is this table still populated?" The raw table is only the beginning.
