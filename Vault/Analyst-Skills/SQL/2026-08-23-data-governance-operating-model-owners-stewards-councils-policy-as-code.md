## What’s new and why it matters
A data governance operating model is the answer to a question every governance program eventually fails to answer on paper: when a metric is wrong, an access grant is too broad, or a PII column ships untagged, who is accountable, who does the work, and what actually stops it from happening again? A policy PDF names none of those people and blocks none of those changes — it sits on a wiki, unread, while the pipeline keeps shipping.
